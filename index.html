Hello World! John Wall Audio Documentary Before the injuries… before the trades… before the questions about what could have been— There was John Wall, the fastest player in the NBA. John Wall didn’t just arrive in basketball. He exploded onto it. Out of Raleigh, North Carolina, Wall was a high school sensation—ranked the number one recruit in the country. His speed was unreal, his handle electric, and his confidence unmistakable. When he chose the University of Kentucky in 2009, expectations were massive—and somehow, he exceeded them. At Kentucky, Wall became a phenomenon. His end-to-end speed changed games. His flair—especially that dance—made him must-see TV. He averaged over 16 points and nearly 7 assists as a freshman, led Kentucky to the Elite Eight, and cemented himself as the clear number one pick in the 2010 NBA Draft. Draft sound effect “With the first pick in the 2010 NBA Draft, the Washington Wizards select… John Wall.” For a franchise desperate for hope, Wall wasn’t just a player—he was a reset button. From day one in Washington, Wall was the engine. He won Rookie of the Year, then grew into one of the leagues elite point guards. At his peak, he was a five-time All-Star, an elite defender, and one of the most explosive playmakers in basketball. The 2016–17 season was the height of it all. Wall averaged 23 points and 11 assists, led the Wizards to a Game 7 in the Eastern Conference semifinals, and hit one of the most iconic shots in franchise history—a buzzer-beating three that sent D.C. into chaos. For a moment, it felt like Washington had arrived—and John Wall was the reason. But speed, the very thing that made Wall special, would soon become the thing his body couldn’t sustain. And thats where the story begins to change.
